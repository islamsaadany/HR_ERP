What changes, and what does not

  • Changes: a small grey i in a circle at the right end of every row. Hovering the row no longer opens the description; hovering the mark does. On hover the mark fills navy so it is clear where the box came from.
  • Stays: the two-column layout, the section headings, the icons, the gold count pills, the row tint on hover, and the dark navy box itself with the attention breakdown in gold.
  • Order on the row: icon · name · count · ⓘ. The mark is always in the same place at the edge, so the eye finds it without reading; the count sits just inside it as today.
