Releasing a cycle’s payments

A finished cycle works out what each person has earned, and there it stops — the figures sit on a report and somebody re-types them somewhere else. This adds the one missing step: release the payments you agree to, and they join the queue Finance already works from, ending at the same bank confirmation as everything else the company pays.

The cycle report’s Compensation by person table gains one button. Each person’s two payable amounts get a status of their own, so you can see at a glance what has gone and what hasn’t.

2 Choosing what to release — this one works

Tick lines and watch each unit’s total. Scheme fees and commission are separate lines, so you can release one without the other. One card per business unit, because each unit banks separately and one release becomes one transaction in one account — there is deliberately no way to put two units in the same release.

One name has no matching employee. A payment can only be released to somebody in the employee registry — that is what says which unit’s account pays it and who confirms it. Fix the spelling in the cycle’s People table and press Recalculate, and the line becomes releasable.

4 Correcting a cycle after some of it has been paid

You can still fix anyone who hasn’t been released. What is refused is an edit that would change a figure already sent — that money has gone, and the report has to keep agreeing with what the bank actually did. The refusal names the person and says which line.

How it behaves

Releasing is not paying

Release puts a payment in Finance’s queue. It is money the company has agreed it owes — nothing has moved. The person is paid when the confirmer marks the transaction complete at the bank, exactly like every other payment the platform handles.

Two lines per person, never merged

Scheme fees and commission are released, sent and confirmed separately, so each traces back to the rule that produced it. You can release one and hold the other.

Nobody is paid twice

A line that has been released disappears from the choosing screen and shows its state on the report instead. Releasing the same amount twice is not something you have to remember not to do — it isn’t offered, and the server refuses it even if two people try at once.

The unit is worked out, never chosen

Which account pays comes from the person being paid, the same way a payback or a benefit claim already works. There is no dropdown to get wrong, and a release can only ever hold one unit’s people.

A name that isn’t an employee stops there

It is grouped, shown and refused — never guessed at. Guessing who M. Sherif is means guessing whose bank account the money leaves from.

The amount is frozen when released

What Finance sends and what the confirmer signs for is the figure as it stood at release, not a figure that could quietly change underneath them.

Two things to confirm

Who may press Release? I’ve assumed you only — a Super User. Finance can already open the incentive module and would see the payments arrive in their queue, but deciding to pay somebody is a different act from making the transaction, and I don’t think the two should sit with the same person. Say if Finance should be able to release too.

Should the person be emailed when it lands? I’ve assumed no. Email is deliberately limited to four workflows, and these are compensation figures. A payback and a benefit claim do email the person on confirmation, so adding this would be consistent — but it would be a fifth workflow, and that is your call, not mine.
