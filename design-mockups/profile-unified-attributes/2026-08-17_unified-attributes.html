Unified attributes on My Profile

One consistent language for who owns each piece of data: a navy pill where HR alone manages a card, a gold mark on the fields you edit yourself, and a “Request a change” button on cards where you propose and HR decides. Plus a new self-edited Legal name field, and dependants broken out into readable rows. Static mockup, matched to the live profile styles.

3 Personal — request button on the card, dependants as rows and now requestable

The “Request a change” button sits on the card itself and opens the request form scoped to this card: date of birth, marital status, and the dependants themselves — the employee can correct a name or date of birth, add a dependant, or remove one. The whole dependant list travels as one proposed change that HR approves or declines in one decision, and HR sees a warning when the employee has a committed medical premium (dependants price the cover). 7 Request status — unchanged, at the bottom

Both card buttons open the same request form you have today, pre-scoped to their card. A submitted request’s status (pending / approved / rejected, with HR’s reason) keeps showing in the existing panel at the bottom of the page — nothing changes there, except its own “Request a change” entry point moves up onto the cards.

Defaults baked into this proposal — veto anything here:
  • Legal name is TWO fields — English and Arabic — each free text, max 120 characters, optional, saved directly by the employee (like Phone), no HR review. The Arabic input types right-to-left. The existing saved legal names stay as the English one.
  • HR sees and can correct both legal names on the admin employee record. Neither appears in the Team Directory.
  • Dependants are requestable as one set: the employee edits the list (correct, add, remove) and it goes to HR as a single “Dependants” change — HR approves or declines the whole list in one decision, alongside any other requested fields.
  • On HR’s review screen, a warning appears when the employee has a committed medical premium, naming the covered people — a dependant change alters who the company insures.
  • Every dependant needs a date of birth; one spouse max — same rules as the HR form.
