Spec 036 · design review

Per-person benefit grants

When the rules block someone from a guaranteed benefit — wrong employment type, under 6 months, no start date — a Super User grants it to that one person for this cycle, with a typed amount. From then on it's the completely normal journey: their Benefits page shows the card, they Request, HR approves, Finance pays, once-per-cycle guard included. Nobody else is affected, and next cycle starts clean.

1 Managing grants — Admin → Benefits → Amounts tab (Super User only)

A new "Individual grants" section under the existing amounts config. Pick the benefit, see its grants, add a person — the picker names why the rules block them, and the amount pre-fills whenever their band can price them.

One grant per person per benefit per cycle · expires when the cycle closes · a grant never changes the benefit's general eligibility · removal is refused once the person has requested it (auditability).

2 What the granted person sees — nothing special, and that's the point

Their Benefits page simply shows the card at THEIR granted amount, and the whole standard journey follows: Request → HR review → Finance payment → the state chips from the once-per-cycle guard.

3 The Release Guaranteed Benefit sheet knows about grants

Granted people appear alongside eligible ones — at their granted amount, marked — so bulk release and the payroll CSV include them. The once-per-cycle guard spans their claims and releases like everyone else's.

Decisions carried in from alignment — veto anything here:
  • Grants handle any blocking reason; the amount is typed per person (pre-filled when the band can price them); this cycle only.
  • Super User manages grants; HR/Finance see the resulting claims and payments as usual.
  • The granted person goes through the normal request → approve → pay flow, with the once-per-cycle guard and state chips.
  • Salary-driven benefits (Loans) excluded. Removal refused once requested. Duplicates prevented.
  • Replaces the reverted Release-sheet typed-amount override. One new table; migration 056.
