  • The working is shown next to every figure — “6 of 12 months”. The expected amount is a claim against the insurer, not an authority, so it has to be checkable at a glance against their credit note.
  • Expected is 13,000, not the 10,834 that was cancelled. December sits inside a charge already applied to the 2026 pool. Reading the cancelled figure would under-claim on every leaver — the single most likely way to build this wrong.
  • A missing last day shows as “Needs leave date”, not a computed number. A confident wrong figure is worse than an obvious gap.
  • The shortfall column is the point of recording actuals: one partial refund is noise, but a column of them says the insurer is systematically short-paying.
  • Write-offs keep their reason, so “we lost it” is always attached to why.
