4 Nothing sends itself

The platform prepares; a person sends. The same rule the holiday announcements already follow, and the reason no scheduled job in this app has ever emailed an employee.

the platformDrafts itA few days ahead, from the date already on record. Words written, unit branding applied.
the platformTells the managerA count on their sidebar, and one nudge email. Never the employee.
the managerEdits itRewrites anything. Adds the gift line, or takes it out.
the managerSends itOne click, to one person. Nothing goes without this.

A draft nobody sends does not go out late. Once the day passes it is marked missed and closed — you cannot wish somebody a happy birthday three days afterwards, and pretending otherwise is worse than silence.

What I’m assuming — correct me and I’ll change it

  • The header and button take the unit's colour; the body does not. Black on white for the message itself, always.
  • The text colour is derived from the brand colour, and the brand is only altered if neither black nor white passes — which happened once in six. Nobody has to think about contrast when choosing a colour.
  • The gold hairline is now the group's — identical on every email, the one constant across units.
  • Announcements are not drafted automatically — only congrats are. An announcement is written when there is something to say.
  • A missed congrats closes rather than sending late.
  • Birthdays state no age. Anniversaries state years, because that is what is being thanked.

Four questions

  1. Which manager? I assume the person's own line manager from the org chart, falling back to HR when somebody has no manager (or when the manager is the one having the birthday). Right?
  2. How far ahead is the draft made? The holidays cron already has a configurable lead — I would give this one the same, defaulting to 3 days.
  3. Is the congrats signed by the manager? It arrives from “People of Forefront Group” either way, but the closing line could read “— Nada”. Warmer, but then it must never send with the manager's name on words they did not read.
  4. Does HR see the queue too? A manager on holiday means a birthday quietly missed. HR seeing all pending drafts, and being able to send one, closes that — at the cost of HR seeing every draft.
