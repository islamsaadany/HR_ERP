Confirmations, one business unit at a time

Today the platform treats confirmation as one company-wide queue. You've asked for it per business unit — each unit banks separately, so each has its own person to confirm. Three screens change. Nothing else about the flow moves: Finance still creates the transactions in the bank, the appointed person still confirms them there, and the people being paid are still told only once that happens.

A unit is decided by who is being paid One submission never mixes two units Salaries: one run per unit per month No confirmer appointed → nothing can be sent, and it says so

Salaries, on the same page
  • The monthly salary run becomes one run per unit — August, that unit's total, that unit's headcount — confirmed by that unit's person.
  • Finance still never sees an individual's pay: a run carries the month, the total, the headcount and the bank reference, and nothing else.
  • A month shows as covered only when every unit's run has been sent, so a unit cannot be quietly forgotten.

2 · The confirmer's screen You, or whoever holds a unit

Unchanged in shape — one list, one button, ten seconds. What changes is that it only ever contains the units that person was appointed for, and each card says which unit it is, so two cards of similar size can't be mistaken for each other.

The rule that stays
  • Holding top-level access does not by itself let you confirm anything — only the people listed against a unit can, and only for that unit.
  • Only a Super User appoints. Appointing yourself is allowed on purpose: it is the way an empty list is never a lock-out.
  • Somebody who leaves the company stops confirming without anyone having to remember to remove them.

Three things I'd like you to check

Before I build it
  • Corrected 25 Aug: Forefront Group is not a business unit, so it has no band and no transactions. The units shown are the ones already set up under Business Units; this feature creates none.
  • A float top-up follows its custodian. The marketing float's top-up is paid to Raneem, so it lands under her unit. If a float should ever belong to a different unit from the person holding it, tell me and I'll make it a property of the float instead.
  • Somebody with no unit is blocked, not guessed at. With no Forefront Group to fall back to, a person with no business unit set is grouped under “No business unit”, shown, and cannot be sent — because guessing a unit would mean guessing which bank account pays them. Setting their unit moves them straight into it.
