What changes

A health check at the top

Each line gets a tick or a warning, and every warning says what to do and links to where you fix it. It checks that sending is set up, that the company’s email address is approved so mail reaches everyone and not only you, that the main switch is on, that both inboxes are filled in, that every business unit has someone to confirm its payments, and whether any email was refused recently.

When everything passes, the card turns green and says “Everything is working”.

A tick on every email

All 18 can be switched off one by one, including the ones that tell people their money has arrived. A tick saves the moment you click it, and nothing else on the page moves. The main switch in Settings still turns everything off at once.

What “off” means

Automatic emails simply aren’t sent; the work itself carries on as normal. The three morning reminders still show their flags on screen. The two you send by hand (holiday announcement, Communications) refuse at their Send button and say they are switched off here.

When it last went out

From the day this ships, the app keeps a record of every email it sends: when, to whom, and whether it was accepted. Older sends won’t appear. A refusal shows in red on its row with the reason, and also in the health check.

A green tick means the email provider accepted it. The app can’t see whether it then landed in someone’s spam folder.

Send yourself a sample

Every row can send you the real email filled in with made-up details, marked “SAMPLE”. This replaces today’s “Send a test email” box, which only sent a plain message.

Inboxes stay in one place

Where an email goes to the HR or Finance inbox, the row shows the address, but you still change it in Settings. Each inbox feeds two emails, so changing it on one row would quietly change the other one too. Where an email goes to a person, the row says who and where that’s decided.

Layout

The page gets wider so the list fits. The order becomes: health check, the list, Settings, then the incentive message. On a phone the list scrolls sideways inside its own box.

Nothing else changes. Who receives each email stays exactly as it is today. This page shows those rules and lets you switch each email off; it doesn’t change them.
