Spec 033 · design review

Profile data request campaigns

HR or Finance picks fields and people; each targeted employee meets one popup on their next visit — empty fields to fill, prefilled ones to confirm or correct — validated exactly like My Profile, written straight to the registry. A sidebar notification keeps count until they finish, and the tracker shows HR who still owes what. Four surfaces below, in the navy/gold language.

1 The popup — what a targeted employee sees on opening the platform

One modal even when several campaigns are open (each group labelled by its campaign title; a field asked twice appears once). Prefilled values carry a green Confirm; editing one replaces it and records “corrected”. “Later” closes it — the sidebar badge stays and the popup returns next visit.

Decisions carried in from alignment — veto anything here:
  • Creators: HR Admin, Finance, Super User. Audience: everyone / departments / picked people, frozen at launch (later hires need a new campaign).
  • Answers write directly to the employee record — registry and CSV update instantly; the tracker is HR's review, not an approval queue. Spec 029's change-request flow is untouched for employee-initiated corrections.
  • Popup always dismissible; the sidebar badge is the persistent reminder. Partial saves fine — only unanswered fields stay pending.
  • All field rules from rounds 1–5 apply (14-digit ID, per-country phone, RTL Arabic, dependant rules). In-app only, no email.
