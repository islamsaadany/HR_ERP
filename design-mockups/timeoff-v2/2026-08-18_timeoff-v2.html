Spec 035 · design review

Time-Off v2 — working days & the complete cycle

Counts become working days (Fridays, Saturdays and listed public holidays never count). No limits — a visible per-year count for the employee, the manager, and HR. Plus: the manager gets a live nav badge, you're warned when you double-book yourself, approved future trips can be cancelled, and requests always follow your current manager. All figures below are illustrative.

A year-count card on top — informational, never a limit. The form previews the working-day cost live as dates are picked, and warns (doesn't block) on self-overlap. Approved future trips get a Cancel.

Add a holiday

Counts are always live: adding a holiday inside an already-approved trip lowers its displayed working-day count. Nothing is deducted from a limit, so nothing needs freezing.

Decisions carried in from alignment — veto anything here:
  • No annual limit — the count informs, never blocks. Counted per calendar year, approved requests only.
  • Weekend = Friday + Saturday; an HR-managed public-holidays list is also excluded. No leave types.
  • Count visible to the employee, the manager on each approval card, and HR (new column).
  • Audit fixes: self-overlap warning (not a block), live manager badge, requests follow the current manager, leavers' pending requests auto-close, employees can cancel an approved future trip.
  • In-app only — no emails (standing rule). No half-days, no team calendar, no types in this round.
