The one rule the table has to keep. A submission is a single transfer from a single unit’s bank account, so two units can never go into one send. Today that is guaranteed by there being no list containing two units. In the table it is guaranteed by the lock above — and the server still refuses a mixed submission on its own, exactly as it does now, because the screen not offering something has never been a control.

Search

Matches the person’s name, what the payment is for, and the amount. Nothing else changes — it narrows the table, it does not clear a selection.

Quick filters

Benefits, Petty cash, Paybacks, Incentive. Each carries its own count, and the counts are of what is actually in the table.

Business unit

A column, and a dropdown to narrow by. Once something is ticked it shows the locked unit instead.

Blocked rows stay visible

A row that cannot be sent — nobody appointed, or the person has no business unit — is still listed, greyed, with the reason on the row rather than a missing button.
