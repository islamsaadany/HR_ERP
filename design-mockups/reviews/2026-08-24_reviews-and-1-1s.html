What I need you to sign off

  • The four-step bar at the top of a sheet — write → both submit → both confirm you met → open & lock.
  • The sealed panel (screen 2): a plain padlock card, no preview of any kind.
  • Two columns side by side once open, rather than one long scroll of both halves.
  • "What you agreed last quarter" as a gold band at the top of the sheet.
  • Three facts — days off, courses, onboarding — as plain tiles, no score.
  • The journal as a list with "Add to review", rather than a text document.
  • The Gallup panel: name-and-date banner first, then the themes, then confirm.

Say which of these to change and I will redraw before anything is built. Colours, type and spacing are the platform's existing navy/gold — unchanged.
