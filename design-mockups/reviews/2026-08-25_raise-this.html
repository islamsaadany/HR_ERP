What I need you to sign off

  • A flag on a journal row, not a separate "incidents" screen — one place to write things down.
  • The gold rail + "To raise" chip for a flagged note.
  • The "N things you flagged to raise" panel at the top of a 1:1, with a Raise it button per line.
  • Carrying it clears the flag automatically and the journal row shows where it went.
  • No badge, no count on the Reviews home, no notification — it appears where the conversation is, and nowhere else.

One thing this deliberately does not do: track whether the issue was resolved. Raising it is the point; what came of it belongs in the 1:1's agreed outcome, which already exists. Say if you want resolution tracked as well and I will draw it.
