Bank confirmations & salaries

Your half of the Finance module. Finance creates the transactions in the bank and submits them here for confirmation; you get an email, confirm them in the bank as you always have, and mark them complete. Nothing in this app releases money — it tells you, and keeps the record of what was done.

Your screen is one list and one button Emails carry totals, never names Benefit reimbursements come through here too Salaries: month, total, headcount — no individual pay

Where you sit in it

Why it's this short
  • No names, no individual amounts. Your choice, and the right one — this reaches you by email, gets forwarded by accident, and stays in the inbox. The link is behind the same sign-in as everything else.
  • The salary email is the same shape: "Salaries for August 2026 — EGP 89,000.00 covering 5 people." Never a person's pay.
  • If something sits unconfirmed for a few days, you get one summary reminder. Nobody else is ever emailed by that.

What to check
  • Nothing here can change while it's waiting on you. If a figure was wrong, Finance has to withdraw the lot and submit fresh — so what you confirm is always what you were emailed.
  • The people in it are told they've been paid the moment you mark it complete, and not before.
  • Benefit claims now come through here too — your note, 2026-08-24. Until now an employee was emailed "you've been reimbursed" when Finance recorded the transfer, which is before the money actually moved. That email now waits for you, like everything else. The trade: a few more lines in your list each month.
