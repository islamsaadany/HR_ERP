4 · Closing the period Finance only

Closing freezes the amounts and carries the balance into next month. It is also the last moment a missing receipt can be caught, so it is the one place the system pushes back.

  • The system lists which lines are missing a receipt rather than showing a generic warning, and records which ones were waved through and by whom — an acknowledgement that doesn't say what was accepted says nothing.
  • A closed period can be reopened with a reason, which re-derives the next month's opening balance.

What to check
  • A request cannot be sent without a receipt — this is the one place evidence is mandatory, because there is no float and no period to reconcile it against later.
  • A decline always carries the reason, shown to the person and emailed to them.
  • "Awaiting payment" becomes "Paid" only when the money has actually gone — which, once spec 040 is in, is when you have approved the transfer.
