Benefits · medical · design review

Age-banded medical — the two surfaces that change, for sign-off

Medical is now priced per person by age (spec 023). Only these two surfaces change shape; the modal's structure is ported faithfully from today's (per-person rows → live premium bar → Commit), recolored/extended, not redesigned. Static mockup — sample data, no wiring.

Sample used throughout: employee Layla, age 32 (band 30–34) · spouse Sara, age 29 (band 25–29) · child Omar, age 10 (band 0–17). Premium = the sum of each covered person's age-band figure. Employees see whole EGP (no cents); HR keeps the exact operator figures on the admin card. Spouse & children are entered in the employee's profile (like kids) — the modal only picks who to cover.
