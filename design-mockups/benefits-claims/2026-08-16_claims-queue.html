Design mockup · Benefits · Admin

Claims queue

Submissions & Claims is currently one unbroken list of cards holding every claim in the cycle — the four that need a decision sit above two hundred that don't, in the same shape and weight. This splits the surface in two: a review queue that only holds decisions, and a ledger that holds everything else. Same statuses, same server rules, same wording for the 50% cap.

  • "Their pool after this" is the missing half of the decision. Approving is a spend against a ceiling, and today the reviewer cannot see the ceiling — Nour's claim would exhaust her pool, and nothing on the current screen says so.
  • Waiting time, sorted oldest-first, replaces a submission date. It answers the only scheduling question HR has.
  • The 50%-cap working moves into the opened row — same sentence, same numbers, shown when someone is actually deciding rather than under every card in the list.
  • Approve stays one click; reject keeps its optional reason but as a permanent inline field, not a details toggle that has to be opened first.
  • Bulk approve for the routine ones. Every claim still runs the same server-side rules — the bar only saves the clicks, never the checks.

The footer total is the point of the ledger: filter to Reimbursed and it answers "what has this cycle actually paid out", which today can only be produced by exporting to a spreadsheet. Record entry keeps its place as a secondary action, and back-filled claims stay tagged so the ledger never implies an employee submitted something they didn't.

What changes, and what doesn't

Leaves the screen

  • One list holding both the queue and the history — decided claims move to the ledger tab.
  • The card layout: amounts that can't be compared down a column, and a status chip that floats to wherever the row wraps.
  • Reject hidden behind a details toggle.
  • The cap working printed under every card — it moves into the opened row.

Stays exactly as it is

  • Four statuses and the spec-020 flow: submit → HR → Finance → employee. No new state.
  • Every server rule: pool ceiling, 50% cap, eligibility — all still evaluated at decision time.
  • The cap sentence word for word: "covers 50% = 7,500 · capped to 6,500 by the 50% benefit cap".
  • Approve, Reject-with-reason, Record entry, and the proof link.
  • Navy/gold, serif headings, the registry's frozen navy table header.

Mockup only — no components changed. Two things here need data the page doesn't load yet: the pool-after-this-claim meter (needs each claimant's ceiling and their claims to date) and the Trail panel (needs the decision timestamps and who decided). Both already exist in the schema; neither is queried by this screen today.
