Built-in safeguards: on touch devices (no hover) the details show by default, keyboard focus reveals them too, and the motion is disabled for “reduce motion” users — so nothing is hidden from anyone who can't hover.

Part 2 · “Your benefits” — status per benefit Option B

Today an employee only sees a claim's status after expanding a benefit. This adds a compact status summary right on the collapsed row, so they know where each benefit stands at a glance. Colors reuse the app's existing claim pills — gold = pending review, navy = reimbursed, red = rejected — with a count each. A benefit with no claims stays clean (no chips).
