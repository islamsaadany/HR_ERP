Medical gate & clamped claims

Two visible changes for sign-off. Everything else in the two fixes is invisible: the pool figure stops counting guaranteed benefits, which corrects a number without moving a pixel. Colours and component shapes are lifted from the live BenefitsBoard.

1

Medical before 3 months

for an employee who already has a tenure band

Today — broken

Medical insurance
Personal medical insurance
Committed once, then locked

→ Employee picks who to cover, commits, and only then is told “Medical insurance becomes available after 3 months of service.”

Proposed Recommended

Medical insurance
Personal medical insurance
Available once you reach 3 months of service
Unlocks at 3 months

→ No “Set up” button, so the dead end is unreachable. Mirrors the existing “Unlocks at 6 months” pill already used for the basket.

  • The alternative is hiding the section outright. I'd keep the locked row — it answers “where is my medical?” instead of leaving a silent gap, and reuses a pattern the page already has.
  • Employees with no start date on record still see medical. That is the existing deliberate fail-safe (an unknown date is treated as eligible rather than locking someone out) — unchanged.

Claiming more than the benefit has left

your gym example, to the figure
Second gym receipt this yearEGP
Full price paid10,000
Company coverage at 80%8,000
Already claimed on gym8,000
Left under the 50% cap (15,000)7,000
Reimbursed — proposed7,000

Effective coverage lands at 70% instead of 80%, and the 50% cap holds exactly. Today this claim is refused outright and the employee is reimbursed nothing.

Today — refused

Company covers 80% → EGP 8,000 reimbursed. Up to EGP 7,000 covered left.

That covered amount exceeds what's left (EGP 7,000). Lower the price or claim the rest later.

Proposed Recommended

Company covers 80% → EGP 8,000 reimbursed. Up to EGP 7,000 covered left.

Only EGP 7,000 is left on this benefit, so you'll be reimbursed EGP 7,000 — not EGP 8,000. Submitting is fine.

  • The gold note replaces the red error and the input stops going red — the claim is valid now, just partially covered, so it shouldn't read as a mistake.
  • The figure is computed server-side and re-checked on submit; the preview only mirrors it, so a stale page can never talk the server into paying more.
  • When a benefit has nothing left, it still refuses — with “You've used the full 50% cap on this benefit”, rather than a clamp to zero.
  • The same clamp applies to the pool ceiling: reimbursement is the smallest of the covered share, what's left on the benefit, and what's left in the pool.
