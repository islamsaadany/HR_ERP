Travel allowance

The summer allowance becomes a year-round travel allowance inside the flexible basket: a fixed band amount, 100% paid, no receipt, drawn from the pool. Two surfaces change — the employee's benefit row and where HR configures the amounts.

✓ EGP 5,000 requested
  • It draws from the pool, as you asked — so a 30,000 pool with a 5,000 travel request leaves 25,000 for everything else.
  • The amount is the band figure, not a ceiling to claim against and not a coverage percentage. One request takes the whole allowance.
  • On a short benefits cycle it prorates with the pool, so a 6-month cycle pays half.
  • Full- and part-timers get the same amount for their band. Their pool ceilings differ, so the same allowance is a larger share of a part-timer's budget — the 50% cap won't intervene, since the band amount sits below it either way.

One amount per tenure band, for full- and part-timers alike — so there is no second row to keep in step, and no way to update one employment type and forget the other. Figures shown are the full-time amounts already in your configuration; the migration copies them across rather than asking you to re-enter them.

  • The flexible catalogue grid (name, category, claim requirement, coverage, status) is untouched — amounts belong in the Amounts tab beside the guaranteed ones, not as eight more columns on an already-wide grid.
  • The old Summer allowance is retired, not deleted: past summer claims are linked to it and would be destroyed with it. It stops appearing for employees and keeps its history.
