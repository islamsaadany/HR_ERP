Benefits Reporting

A read-only cycle overview for HR/Finance: every figure computed by the same engine that enforces claims (pending claims reserve pool money; guaranteed benefits sit outside the pool; medical counts what THIS cycle absorbs). Click a person → the full story in a popup. Download the whole table as a formatted Excel.

1 The page — Admin → Benefits → Reporting

Cycle picker (defaults to the open cycle) · summary tiles that always equal the table's sums · sortable/filterable full-width table · Download Excel in the house workbook style. Rows are clickable.

Decisions carried in from alignment — veto anything here:
  • Access: HR Admin, Finance, Super User. Read-only — nothing on this page mutates benefits.
  • Pool math identical to the claim engine: pending claims count in used/remaining AND get their own column; guaranteed benefits shown separately, never in the pool; medical = this cycle's charge.
  • Cycle picker defaults to the open cycle; every figure recomputes per cycle under that cycle's own cap setting.
  • Leavers excluded by default (a filter can include them); unconfigured employees listed with "No pool" rather than hidden.
  • Download Excel: the current table in the house workbook style (navy header, frozen panes, filters, dd/mm/yyyy).
