One thing to decide for the wide tables

This works cleanly here because the nine columns fit the screen. The Employees registry and the Benefits catalogue have far more columns and need to scroll sideways with a frozen first column — and CSS will not let a box scroll sideways while its header sticks to the page. Those two can have one or the other, not both. Everything above is unaffected by that choice.
