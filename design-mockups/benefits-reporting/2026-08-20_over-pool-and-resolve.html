Finding and fixing an over-drawn pool

Three things to sign off here: how an over-drawn row reads, how you find them all, and what HR does about one. The server now refuses to create a new overrun — this is for the ones already in the data, and for the ones a future ceiling change creates.

Why any exist at all. The ceiling can still shrink under money already spent — lowering a configured ceiling, changing someone's employment type or start date, or re-dating a cycle. Those have to stay possible for HR, so they are surfaced here rather than blocked.

Every route is written to the claim/commitment history with who did it and when. Money is never moved silently — if Finance has already reimbursed a claim you reduce here, the difference shows in the Payments queue as a recovery.

One thing to confirm

Option 2 (raise her ceiling for this cycle) is a per-person exception, which the codebase already has a home for — the Super-User grants used for exceptional releases (spec 036). I would reuse that rather than invent a second exception mechanism, which means Super User only, while the other three routes stay open to HR Admin. Say if you would rather all four were HR.
