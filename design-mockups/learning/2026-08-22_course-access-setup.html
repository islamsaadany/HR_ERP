Who takes this course

Rewritten after your note: this is a course setup, not a list of routes you build one by one. You answer one question — who takes this? — and the system works out the rest. The word “route” disappears from the screen, and the table with it. Illustrative content throughout.

1 The two switches, told apart

These get confused because they sound similar. They are independent, and Draft always wins — a Draft course marked “Everyone” is still seen by nobody.

Draft / Published

Does this course exist for anybody yet?

Draft Invisible to every employee, even on a direct link. Only course authors see it.

Published It is real. Now the second switch decides who.

Lives on the course header, beside the title. Unchanged.

Everyone / Only certain people

Once published, who can see it?

Everyone Every active employee.

Only certain people Only who you choose below.

Lives at the top of this tab. Renamed from “Open / Restricted”, which said the same thing in words nobody uses out loud.

2 The setup the whole tab

Seven named fields instead of a build-a-row box. Each is a list of what you have chosen, with how many people it covers right now, and a + add that opens a searchable tick-list. Nothing is a table, and nothing saves until you press Add.

4 What goes away

Four things removed, and the reason each earned its removal.

removed The second “Everyone” — the Audience dropdown’s first entry. Today you can set a course to Restricted and then pick “Everyone” there, so the badge says Restricted while every employee gets it. One control now, at the top.

removed The routes table — it repeated what the fields now say, in a form that needed a glossary. Individuals live on the People tab.

removed The word “route” — jargon I introduced. It stays in the code, where it is the right word.

fixed The people counts. Every line currently shows the same figure — everyone matched by any of your choices — so Consulting (9) and Endurance (0) both read 23. Each is now counted on its own, which is the only way the gold “reaches nobody” flag can work.

What I’m assuming — correct me and I’ll change it

  • The upload template is untouched. Access is set here, as you said.
  • Draft/Published stays exactly where it is on the course header. Only this tab changes.
  • “Open / Restricted” is renamed to “Everyone / Only certain people”. Same setting, same behaviour, words people say out loud.
  • Every field is one kind of thing. You do not mix people and departments in one Add — each field adds its own kind, which is what makes the counts meaningful.
  • Groups are still made on Manage groups. This tab points at them; it is not a second place to edit who is in one.
  • Nothing is lost. Existing courses keep every choice they have — the same data, shown as fields instead of rows.
  • Mid-course people are not shown here. Someone who started a course and then stopped matching keeps it until they finish; that is on the People tab, marked as such, because it is not something you choose.
