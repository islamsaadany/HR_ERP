Spec 038 · learning manager

Someone who runs Learning, and nothing else

A person who can build and run courses without becoming an HR Admin. They reach only the Learning part of the admin — nothing about salaries, benefits, leave or employee records. HR appoints them from a new Setup page inside Learning, and stays above it. Illustrative content throughout.

1 Setup: who runs Learning HR only

A new page at Admin → Learning → Setup, beside “Manage groups”. HR Admins and Super Users are listed but not removable — they hold Learning because they hold everything, and pretending otherwise would create a way to lock HR out of a module.

These people can reach Admin → Learning. Nothing else in the admin opens for them.

3 The part I need you to decide open

You said HR “becomes on verification level or something”. That could mean two quite different products, and it changes how much gets built — so I have drawn both rather than picking for you.

A · HR oversees, doesn’t gate simpler

The manager publishes courses themselves. HR’s “verification” is that they appointed the person, can see everything they do, and can remove them in one click.

Sara builds a course → Sara publishes it → employees see it.

Fastest to run day to day. Nothing waits on HR. The risk is that a half-finished course can go live without a second pair of eyes.

B · HR signs off before it goes live more to build

The manager does everything up to publishing, then sends it for approval. HR gets a queue, the same way suggested resources work now.

Sara builds a course → Sent for approval → HR publishes → employees see it.

A real second pair of eyes, and a record of who approved what. The cost is that a course sits waiting whenever HR is busy — the thing you were trying to get away from.

My recommendation: A, and add B later if it turns out to be needed. You are appointing someone precisely so training stops queueing behind HR; putting a queue back in at the last step undoes most of that. Everything a manager does is already visible to HR — the course list, the roster, who published what — so “oversight” is real without being a gate.

What I’m assuming — correct me and I’ll change it

  • It is not a new role on the employee record. Sara stays an EMPLOYEE; she holds an appointment to run Learning. That is why it cannot leak into Benefits or salaries — there is nothing to leak, and the employee form does not change.
  • HR Admins and Super Users keep Learning automatically, are shown on the list as “always”, and cannot be removed from it — otherwise the module could be locked away from HR.
  • A learning manager cannot appoint another one. They see the list read-only.
  • The roster is included — names, courses, progress. You cannot run training blind to who has done it. No pay, leave, or personal data.
  • Appointing is per person, not per course. A manager runs the whole module, not a subject area. Per-course ownership is a bigger idea and I would rather not guess at it.
  • Nothing about the module switch changes — if Learning is switched off in Modules, it is off for managers too.
