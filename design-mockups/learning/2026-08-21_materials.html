Course documents & resources

Two different things on one new tab. Documents are the standard set you attach to every course — outline, expanded outline, slides — and only the slides reach employees. Resources are the reading and links you recommend alongside it, and employees see all of them. Illustrative content throughout.

1 A fourth tab HR

Content · Access · People · Materials. Documents are three fixed slots rather than a free-for-all list, because they are a standard — a course either has its outline or it doesn't, and a named slot makes that visible at a glance instead of making you read filenames.

What I'm assuming — correct me and I'll change it

  • Three fixed document slots — Outline, Expanded outline, Slides. Not a free file list. Adding a fourth kind later is a small change; letting people upload anything they like is the thing that's hard to undo.
  • Only Slides reach employees. The other two are HR-only and are not hinted at anywhere on the employee side.
  • Resources have four types — Book, Article, Course, Link — with a title, an optional link, and a one-line note. A book needs no URL, which is why the link is optional.
  • Materials belong to the COURSE, not a lesson, so they sit once at the bottom of the course rather than repeating under every lesson.
  • Resources appear even before anyone has started — they are a reason to start, not a reward for finishing.

Two things I'd particularly like a yes or no on: should slides download or preview in the page, and should a Course resource be a real link to another course here (so it moves if that course is renamed) rather than typed text?
