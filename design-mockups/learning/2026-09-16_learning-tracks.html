Learning tracks

Five screens. A track is a named path of courses in a deliberate order — you build it once and hand it to a person or a whole group, and being on it is what gives them the courses. Everything below is drawn from the agreed spec; nothing is built yet.

1 The tracks you have

A new entry inside Learning, beside the courses. Each row says what the path is for and how many people are on it — that count is the whole point of the row, so it is counted the same way the real check counts.

Settings

Chase overdue courses by email When a deadline passes with a course unfinished, the person is emailed and their manager is told. At most five messages about one course — one on the day, then weekly for four weeks — then it stops arriving, though it stays marked overdue on every screen. Anyone who runs Learning can switch this off. Switching it back on needs an HR Admin.

Drawn on. Off by default until somebody turns it on deliberately.

Two things this switch deliberately does not do. It cannot turn email on when the platform's own master switch is off — it can only ever silence, never enable. And switching it off hides the emails, not the problem: an overdue course still reads overdue to the employee, their manager and you.
