Course builder — two more contained layouts

Same content, same controls, less height. Both options collapse the four stacked header rows into one, put Minutes · Must watch · Counts toward progress on a single line, and hide Notes behind a disclosure. They differ in where the lesson settings live. Figures are illustrative.

B Settings on the lesson row

Same compact header. Which one?

Both do everything you asked: one header line instead of four, the three small settings on one line, Notes collapsed, and the Published chip sharing a row with Return to draft.

  • A — recommended. Everything about a lesson stays in one place. Cheapest to build, nothing moves that you already know the position of.
  • B is shorter still, but you edit a lesson's title in the left column and its video in the right, which is a harder thing to learn.

Also in both, worth a yes/no: Checkpoints open by default (they're rarer, but when present they matter), Notes always start closed, and the labels shrink to Minutes · Must watch · Progress. Say if you'd rather keep the longer wording.
