Putting the courses in order

Two changes to Admin → Learning, and nothing else on the screen moves. The list gains a row of quick filters, and each course gains a handle you drag. The order you set for the published courses is the order employees meet them in.

Changed since v2: the handle is now the only way to move a course — the move up / move down entries are gone from the ⋯ menu, as asked. Nothing replaces them on the row. Instead the handle answers to all three ways of holding the machine: drag it with a mouse, press and hold to lift it on a phone, or give it keyboard focus and use the up and down keys.

1 Quick filters instead of silent grouping

Today the list is sorted by state with nothing saying so — drafts first, then published, then paused, with no heading between them. The three states become filters you can press, and the count on each tells you how many there are before you press it.

2 Drag the handle — mid-move

Pressing “Published” gives you the list you will actually arrange in. Take a course by the handle and the row lifts; a navy line shows where it will land; everything below shuffles down to make room. Let go and it stays — no Save button, the drop is the save.

It cannot leave its group

A published course only ever drops among published courses — the line will not appear over the drafts. Publishing a draft puts it at the bottom of the published list, where you drag it up.

Nobody’s progress moves

Ordering touches the list and nothing else. Every tick, every watched second and every completion stays exactly where it is.

If the drop doesn’t save

The row snaps back to where it was and says so, rather than sitting in a new place that only you can see.

3 One handle, however you are holding the machine

The ⋯ menu is back to what it is today — rename and delete, nothing added. The handle carries the whole job instead, which means it has to answer to more than a mouse.

What the handle answers to

Mouse — drag it, as drawn above.

Phone — press and hold, then move. The hold is what separates lifting a course from scrolling the page, so the two never fight; it is the gesture phones already use for rearranging.

Keyboard — tab to the handle and press up or down. Without this a course cannot be moved at all without a mouse, and the rest of the admin screens are reachable by keyboard.

4 Where the order actually shows up

This is the reason the feature is worth having, and also its one honest limit: an employee’s page splits into what they still owe and what they have finished. Your order runs inside each half, not across the split.
