Putting the courses in order

Two changes to Admin → Learning, and nothing else on the screen moves. The list gains a row of quick filters, and each course gains a pair of small arrows that move it up or down. The order you set for the published courses is the order employees meet them in.

1 Quick filters instead of silent grouping

Today the list is sorted by state with nothing saying so — drafts first, then published, then paused, with no heading between them. The three states become filters you can press, and the count on each tells you how many there are before you press it.

2 Pressing “Published” — the ordering view

The filter you will actually live in. Same rows, same arrows, nothing but the published courses in the order employees meet them. The number on the left is that course’s place in the list, and it renumbers itself as you move things.

One press, one move

A course swaps with its neighbour and the list renumbers itself. No Save button — the move is the save, the same way renaming a course already works.

It cannot reach across a state

A published course only ever moves among published courses. Publishing a draft drops it at the bottom of the published list, where you can walk it up.

Nobody’s progress moves

Ordering touches the list and nothing else. Every tick, every watched second and every completion stays exactly where it is.

3 Where the order actually shows up

This is the reason the feature is worth having, and it is also its one honest limit: an employee’s page splits into what they still owe and what they have finished. Your order runs inside each half, not across the split.
