Later, not now: once a course has more than a handful of resources, group them by type (Books · Articles · Videos · Courses) with the newest first. Building that today would be scaffolding around three items.

What I'm assuming — correct me and I'll change it

  • Slides preview in the page, full-screen on demand. This works natively for a PDF; a PowerPoint file cannot preview in a browser, so a .pptx would fall back to a download button. I'd accept both and preview what can be previewed.
  • A resource is type + name + optional link — Book, Article, Course, Video, Link. No note field; the name carries it.
  • Rating is 1–5 stars, optional, once per completion. HR sees the average and the count per course, never who rated what.
  • A suggestion is attached to the course the person just finished, and HR can edit it before approving. Declining is silent.
  • Materials are a curriculum entry, not a lesson — no progress, no completion, always visible, doesn't affect anyone's percentage.
  • Employees suggest only at completion, not from a button that's always there — it keeps the queue honest and ties the suggestion to a course.

Three things I'd like a yes or no on: (a) ratings anonymous to HR as above, or should HR see who said what? (b) can an employee suggest a resource any time from the course page too, not only at the end? (c) should a renewal — retaking a course a year later — ask for the rating again?
