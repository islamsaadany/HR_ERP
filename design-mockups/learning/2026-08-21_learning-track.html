2 Adding required content to a finished course HR

Your Q1 answer, made visible. Adding a required lesson to a published course that people have already completed asks before it does anything — and the server refuses the edit if the answer never arrives, so this can’t be skipped.

3 people have already completed this course

You’re adding a required lesson. Does that reopen the course for them?

Reopen it for them They drop back to 92% and the new lesson appears in their list. Their original completion date is kept and shown alongside.
Leave their completion standing They stay complete. The new lesson isn’t required of them.
Counts only people the course still reaches — someone who finished it and has since moved out of Consulting isn’t pulled back in.

What I need signed off

  • The three-tab builder (Content · Access · People) rather than separate pages — matches Benefits Management, keeps “who gets this” next to “what is it”.
  • Route as a first-class column on the roster, with grandfathered rows tinted gold and withdrawable.
  • My learning framed as obligations, not a catalogue — no browse, no search, no enrol.
  • The reopen dialog’s wording and the two choices — this is the one place an HR edit changes someone’s finished record.
  • Gate copy on the player — stated as a fact (“you’ve watched 62%… watch 80% to complete”) rather than a blocked-action error.

Colour follows the house rule: navy = action, green = done only, gold = needs attention or in-review, red = destructive or refused.
