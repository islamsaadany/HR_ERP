Spec 038 · access tab

Choosing who takes a course

The template stays as it is — no “who it’s for” sheet. Instead the Access tab does the job properly: one control instead of three, pick several at once, search for a person by typing, and nothing commits until you press Add. Illustrative content throughout.

1 Today it is three controls that behave differently

An audience needs two dropdowns and a button. A group applies the instant you pick it. A person applies the instant you pick it. Three mechanisms, two of them with no way back except Remove — and every one of them takes exactly one item.

Adding five people means five trips through a dropdown of every employee in the company, each one saving the moment you release the mouse.

2 One control, several at a time proposed

Pick what kind of route, tick as many as you want, press Add. The same shape whether you are adding two departments, three groups, or eight people — and you can see what you are about to add before it happens.

Someone who already has access is shown but not selectable, so you cannot add the same person twice and then wonder why there are two rows. Nothing is saved until you press the button — the current panel saves the moment a dropdown changes, which is why there is no way to change your mind.

3 The routes table, saying what it is supposed to say

Each route reports its own reach — see the defect below, which this fixes. A route that reaches nobody is called out in gold rather than looking identical to one that works, and a group row opens to show who is actually in it instead of sending you to another screen.

Unchanged: the Open/Restricted switch above the table, and the People tab, which is still where you go to see every individual the course reaches and how far they have got.

A defect I found while reading this panel

Every audience row currently shows the same number: everyone matched by any audience rule, not by that rule. So “Consulting” and “Endurance” both read 23 even when Endurance reaches nobody — which is precisely the case the column exists to expose. There is also a second query (audienceReach) run on every page load whose result is never used.

Both are fixed as part of this: each rule counted on its own, and the dead query removed. Worth knowing it has been wrong since the tab shipped — any “this reaches N people” figure you have read on an audience row was the combined total.

What I’m assuming — correct me and I’ll change it

  • The template is unchanged. No “who it’s for” sheet, as you said — access is set here.
  • One kind at a time, many values at once. You pick “Specific people” then tick eight; you do not mix people and departments in one Add. Mixing reads clever and is harder to check before you commit.
  • Search appears where a list is long — people, departments, managers. A list of two business units does not need a search box.
  • Each tick becomes its own route row, exactly as today: three departments make three removable rows, not one lumped “3 departments”.
  • Groups are still created and edited on Manage groups. This panel points at them and shows who is in them; it does not become a second place to edit membership.
  • Nothing else on the tab moves — the Open/Restricted switch, the routes-add-up note, and the People tab all stay as they are.
