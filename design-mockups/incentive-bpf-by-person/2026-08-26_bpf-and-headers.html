2 The header change, on its own

You asked for the title to take the line with the subtitle beneath it. That is the section header every section in the report shares, so the change lands on all of them — which is the point: they should read the same way. It is worth seeing what it fixes.

Today

Compensation by person released compensation, plus commission folded into the grand total

The subtitle fights the button for the same line, so it is cut off — and the longer the title, the less of it survives.

Proposed

Total compensation by person released compensation, plus commission folded into the grand total

Title on its own line, subtitle in full beneath it. The longer name now fits, and so does every other section’s.

How the new table behaves

It is the same figure, split open

A person’s Business Partner Fee is their lead fees plus their contributor payments — exactly the Total column of the table below it. Nothing new is calculated; the same derivation is shown per person instead of per client.

The breakdown says which hat

Each line is tagged Lead or Contributor, because the two are earned by different rules. A lead line carries the client’s margin and the envelope it generated; a contributor line carries the share and the tier it fell in.

A zero still says why

A contribution that fell below the 5%-of-month floor shows as 0 (floored) rather than vanishing — the same treatment the fee tables already give it. A person who earned nothing at all is simply not listed.

Sorted by what was earned

Largest first, like Commission by person, with a total row that reconciles to the Business Partner Fee section’s own totals — lead fees plus contributor payments.

One thing to confirm. Renaming that section to Total compensation by person also changes the sheet name inside the downloadable .xlsx, which is built from the same words. That keeps the workbook and the screen agreeing, which is what you want — but anybody with an old workbook open will see the old name. Say if you would rather the file kept its current wording.
