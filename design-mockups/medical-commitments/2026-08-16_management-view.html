Medical commitments — management view

The same figures HR reads today, re-shaped so a cycle with 30+ commitments can be scanned instead of read. Nothing is removed: the per-cycle charge split, the reconciliation sentences, re-price and remove all survive — they move from "always on every row" to "on the row you opened". Three things are new: a totals rail, an exceptions filter, and the list of people who have not committed yet.

Why this moved out of the row

Today every commitment carries live dependant checkboxes one click away from Re-price, in a list HR is scrolling. Medical is the locked, money-bearing benefit — changing who is covered should be a decision, not a stray click.

The panel also shows the working: each covered person's age, the band it lands in, and the figure it contributes. Right now the premium is a single number and the only way to check it is to re-derive it from the rate card.

Re-price reports what changed before it's applied ("was 18,352 → 16,252"), so an accidental un-tick is visible rather than silent.

A missing date of birth blocks the commit outright (spec 023), and today nothing on this screen says so — the person simply never appears. There is no "send a reminder" button here on purpose: email stays limited to the claim workflow (spec 020). This is a chase list HR works from, not a notification surface.

6

What changes, and what doesn't

Leaves the screen

  • The permanently-expanded "Charged to" box under every row — it moves behind the row you open.
  • The repeated "EGP N falls after 2026 ends" sentence — it becomes the Carries over column, and stays as prose only for the exceptions.
  • Always-live dependant checkboxes in the list — they move into the Manage panel.

Stays exactly as it is

  • Every figure: premium, term dates, per-cycle months and amounts, charge statuses, committed date.
  • All four wordings for over-charge, cover-ended recovery, closed-cycle freeze, and carry-over.
  • Re-price, Re-price all, Remove, Export CSV — same actions, same server rules.
  • Navy/gold, the serif headings, the frozen navy table header from the registry.

Mockup only — no components changed. Separate from this: on the live screen every dependant checkbox renders with the same id="adminbenefits-dependantIds" inside the row loop, and its label points at that id, so clicking a label's text toggles the first row's checkbox instead of its own. Worth fixing whether or not this redesign goes ahead.
