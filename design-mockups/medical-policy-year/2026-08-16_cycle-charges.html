Medical cycle charges

The medical policy runs 1 Jun 2026 – 31 May 2027 while the benefits cycle runs Jan – Dec. The employee commits to the whole premium once; each cycle's pool absorbs only its months. Two surfaces change — the employee's pool card and HR's commitment list.

Cover ended 30 Nov 2026. The remaining EGP 10,834 was never charged to a pool, and the premium paid in advance for cover after that date is recovered from the insurer — so nothing is owed and nothing is written off. Recoverable period: 1 Dec 2026 – 31 May 2027, which includes one month still sitting inside the 2026 charge above.
  • The committed premium keeps the position and prominence it has today; the per-cycle table is added beneath it, so nothing HR currently reads has moved.
  • Applied means it is drawing against that cycle's pool now. On cycle open means it will, automatically, when HR opens that cycle — no action needed. Not charged means cover ended before that cycle, so the charge was cancelled — the advance premium comes back from the insurer rather than being owed by anyone.
  • Totals always reconcile to the committed premium. If a row ever fails to add up, that is a bug, and showing the total is how it becomes visible rather than silent.
